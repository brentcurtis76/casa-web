Using the project

Terms of use

Mail Desk is a personal Linux desktop mail prototype maintained by Brent Curtis. These terms describe its intended use and current limits.

Updated 14 October 2026 · Current read-only prototype

Intended use

Use Mail Desk only with Google accounts you own or are authorized to access, and respect your organization’s account rules. Access is granted through Google’s consent process. This website provides project and privacy information; it does not offer a public installer, registration, subscription, or account connection.

Current scope

The Gmail integration is for reading and synchronization only. It does not send mail or submit mailbox changes. Calendar, other providers, and provider-backed writes are outside the current version. The separate offline demonstration uses synthetic messages and local actions.

The recent-mail cache can contain partial conversations. Messages without saved bodies need a connection before their content can be read. Bodies larger than 2 MB are refused rather than truncated, and unavailable headers may leave incomplete coverage. The app is not a complete mailbox backup.

Prototype availability and reliability

The software is under development. Offline tests are complete, but live account compatibility, recovery, and long-term token behavior are still being validated. Bugs, service outages, account restrictions, expired authorization, and cache failures may interrupt access or produce an incomplete local view. No uninterrupted service, recovery guarantee, or support response time is promised.

Keep important messages and backups in their original services or other appropriate storage. The current mail cache is not encrypted by the app. Protect the computer and any copies of its files.

Google services and authorization

Google’s terms and account policies continue to apply when you use Gmail and grant access. Mail Desk is an independent personal project; it is not a Google product and does not claim Google endorsement or verification. Your organization may restrict third-party access.

You can remove Mail Desk’s access through your Google Account connections. Signing out locally and removing Google authorization do not remove cached mail. See the privacy policy for the separate data-removal steps.

Privacy and support

The privacy policy explains data access, storage, retention, diagnostics, and control. For questions about the project, these terms, or privacy, contact brentcurtis76@gmail.com. Do not include credentials or private messages unless specifically needed and agreed.

Changes

The project may change as validation progresses. These terms will be updated to describe material changes to use or availability. New Google permissions or new uses of Google data require an explicit authorization process; accepting these terms does not authorize additional scopes.
